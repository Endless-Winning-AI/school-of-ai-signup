A Gift from Diana

Good Morning, Holy Spirit

A morning declaration to welcome the Holy Spirit into every decision of the day.

IThe Invitation

Good morning Holy Spirit — you say in the book of James that you are yearning for me to welcome you in to every part of my decision-making day, that you have an intense longing and intense desire and craving for me to welcome you, to be acknowledged daily.

You have said it is your sincere willingness, your great willingness, that will move to action, will make come alive and produce my deepest thoughts and deepest desires. That you are pouring out your kindness, your tenderness, your affection and love upon me. Your great grace!!! Great grace, great grace — unprecedented favor in ways that I have never known, never tasted before.

I come to receive this. I come to receive fresh abilities, empowerments, capabilities that I have never had before or never known before. I thank you Holy Spirit, I thank you for this great grace, this great grace!

IIThe Surrender

Therefore Holy Spirit, I welcome you today! I give you my free will and boldly say: take over my mind! Have your full and entire way within me today! Give me clear, clear direction today and fresh inspiration! Help me make the right choices today!

IIIThe Thanksgiving

I thank you that you are directing, you are ordering my steps with great clarity! I yield to you with great excitement knowing that you have ordained me to be an astounding success. I stand in awe of your great grace! I thank you God that my latter will be much greater, supernaturally above my former!

I thank you that I am mentally stimulated to do what you have called me to do — highly motivated, highly creative, highly focused, highly organized and disciplined! With fresh powers and abilities!

I thank you Holy Spirit for fresh wisdom, fresh understanding — that you are enlarging my capacity to receive and understand your ways and comprehend you in ways I’ve never known before!

I thank you Holy Spirit that you are swallowing up all wrong thinking, all toxic thinking — that your light is obliterating all darkness in every realm of my life!

I thank you Holy Spirit for answers, solutions, sudden brilliant creative timely ideas! A-ha moments! Clever ideas!

I thank you Holy Spirit that you have appointed me and anointed me, decreed over me and installed me, established me to rule and reign in outstanding success!

I thank you Holy Spirit for supernatural Kingdom connections, divinely orchestrated from heaven by you Holy Spirit. How I thank you. How I thank you. How I thank you.
