01

The Oracle Prompt

This single prompt gives you $500/hour expert advice for free. Copy it, paste it into ChatGPT or Claude, and replace the brackets with your situation.

You are a [expert type] from the future with 50 years of experience looking back at this exact moment in time. I'm going to describe my situation. I want you to: 1. Ask the questions a true expert would ask before giving advice 2. Share one counterintuitive insight that most people in my position miss 3. Tell me the ONE thing that will matter most 5 years from now 4. Give me my next 3 actions, in order of priority My situation: [describe your situation here]

How to use it: Replace [expert type] with the kind of advisor you need (business strategist, marketing expert, financial planner, etc.) and [describe your situation] with your actual situation. Be specific.

02

The Pre-Prompt Checklist

Before every prompt, answer these 4 questions. Takes 30 seconds. Saves 10 minutes of back-and-forth.

  1. 1.
    What do I actually want?
    Be specific. Not "help with marketing" but "5 Instagram post ideas for my painting business targeting homeowners in Tampa."
  2. 2.
    What context does AI need?
    Who are you? What's the situation? "I'm a barber shop owner with 3 chairs in downtown Orlando targeting men 25-40."
  3. 3.
    What does "good" look like?
    Format, tone, length. "I want a professional email under 200 words with a friendly tone."
  4. 4.
    What constraints exist?
    What to avoid, must-haves. "Don't use slang. Must include a call to action."
