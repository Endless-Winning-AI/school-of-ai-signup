## 2. The AI Mindset

- AI is a **skill set** — not magic, not a fad
- Two things to learn:
  - **A way of thinking** — how to approach problems with AI in mind
  - **A tool set** — the actual platforms, apps, and workflows
- This applies to **every industry** — not just for developers or tech people
- Even if you hire someone to do AI work, you need to understand the basics
  - You can't manage what you don't understand
  - Knowing the fundamentals protects you from overpaying, bad advice, and missed opportunities

---

## 3. What AI Is Good At

- Repetitive, pattern-based tasks
- Drafting and iterating (copy, emails, outlines, code)
- Summarizing and extracting info from large amounts of content
- Following instructions precisely when given clear direction
- Working fast — doing in minutes what takes hours manually

---

## 4. What AI Is Not Good At

- Judgment calls that require deep context about *your* business
- Original creative vision — it can execute, but you still set the direction
- Knowing what to ask for (that's your job — the thinking part)
- Anything requiring real-world accountability

---

## 5. AI Is an Assistant, Not Autopilot

- We're not at "set it and forget it" — we're at **"let it assist"**
- Think of AI like a team of interns — capable, fast, but they need direction
- The real unlock: **multiple agents working for you in parallel**
  - While you're on a call, AI is drafting something
  - While you're reviewing one output, another agent is researching the next thing
  - You become the **operator** — directing work instead of doing all of it yourself
- The skill is learning how to manage that workflow, not just use one tool

## 7. Skills & Repeatable Tasks

- The concept of **skills** — packaging a task so AI can repeat it perfectly every time
- Examples of what makes a good repeatable task:
  - Clear inputs and outputs
  - A consistent process you do regularly
  - Something you'd write instructions for if handing it to an employee
- Think about your own work: what do you do over and over that follows a pattern?
