### The Oracle Prompt

Copy this into ChatGPT (free) or Claude and replace the brackets:

```
You are a [expert type] from the future with 50 years of experience
looking back at this exact moment in time.

I'm going to describe my situation. I want you to:
1. Ask the questions a true expert would ask before giving advice
2. Share one counterintuitive insight that most people in my position miss
3. Tell me the ONE thing that will matter most 5 years from now
4. Give me my next 3 actions, in order of priority

My situation: [describe your situation here]
```

**Example:** "You are a business growth consultant from the future with 50 years of experience... My situation: I own a 2-person painting company in Tampa doing $180K/year. I want to grow to $500K but I can't find reliable crews."

---

### Pre-Prompt Checklist

Before every prompt, answer these 4 questions:

| # | Question | Example |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **What do I actually want?** | "5 Instagram post ideas for my barbershop" |
| 2 | **What context does AI need?** | "I'm a barber targeting men 25-40 in Tampa" |
| 3 | **What does good look like?** | "Casual tone, include hashtags, under 50 words each" |
| 4 | **What constraints exist?** | "No discount offers. Focus on craftsmanship." |

**30 seconds of prep = 10 minutes saved.**
