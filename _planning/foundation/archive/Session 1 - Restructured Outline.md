### Section 1: The Oracle Prompt -- TTFV (0:00-6:00)

**Format:** Teach 1 min > Demo 3 min > Interactive 2 min
**Speaker:** Troy

**TEACH (1 min)**

Cold open. No housekeeping. No introductions.

> "$500/hour advice in 30 seconds. For free. I'm going to show you something, then you're going to try it yourself."

**DEMO (3 min)**

Troy demos the Oracle Prompt live using a relatable business scenario (auto detailing owner in Tampa).

**The Oracle Prompt:**

```
You are a [expert type] from the future with 50 years of experience looking back at this exact moment in time.

I'm going to describe my situation. I want you to:
1. Ask the questions a true expert would ask before giving advice
2. Share one counterintuitive insight that most people in my position miss
3. Tell me the ONE thing that will matter most 5 years from now
4. Give me my next 3 actions, in order of priority

My situation: [describe]
```

- Have ChatGPT tab pre-loaded. Type the prompt live (or paste -- it should look effortless).
- Narrate what AI returns. React naturally. "See that? That's not generic advice. That's specific to THIS situation."

**INTERACTIVE (2 min)**

- Drop the Oracle Prompt template in chat.
- "Copy that. Paste it into ChatGPT or Claude right now. Replace the brackets with YOUR situation. You have 90 seconds."
- After 90 seconds: "Raise your hand if you got something that surprised you."

**TAKEAWAY:**

> "If this is all you do tonight, you're ahead of 95% of people using AI."

**TRANSITION:** "Now let me show you WHY that worked. Because most people skip this part."

### Section 2: The Skill Framework (6:00-14:00)

**Format:** Teach 5 min > Demo 3 min
**Speaker:** Troy

**TEACH (5 min)**

**Hunter Analogy (2 min):**

> "Think about a hunter with a rifle. A skilled hunter can take down a deer, an elk, maybe even a moose with the right weapon. But give that same skilled hunter a 9mm handgun and ask them to take down a moose? Wrong tool. Wrong job. Doesn't matter how good they are."
>
> "Now flip it. Give a complete beginner a perfect rifle, custom scope, the works. They'll still miss."
>
> "AI works exactly the same way."

Project visual:

| | Right Tool | Wrong Tool |
|---|---|---|
| **High Skill** | Great results | Wasted effort |
| **Low Skill** | Missed potential | Nothing works |

**Formula: AI Mastery = Skill + Tool Selection**

**GIGO (1.5 min):**

> "AI is pattern prediction, not thinking. It predicts the most likely next word based on billions of text examples. That's why it sounds confident even when it's wrong."
>
> "Garbage in, garbage out. Your input determines your output."

**Pre-Prompt Checklist (1.5 min):**

> "Before every prompt, ask yourself four questions. This takes 30 seconds. It saves 10 minutes."

Project the checklist:

1. **What do I actually want?** (be specific)
2. **What context does AI need?** (who am I, what's the situation)
3. **What does "good" look like?** (format, tone, length)
4. **What constraints exist?** (what to avoid, must-haves)

**DEMO (3 min)**

Side-by-side: Weak prompt vs Strong prompt. Same AI, same free account.

| Weak Prompt | Strong Prompt |
|---|---|
| "Write me a marketing email" | "Write a marketing email for my auto detailing business in Tampa targeting luxury car owners. Tone: professional but friendly. Include a limited-time offer for ceramic coating. Keep it under 200 words." |

- Have BOTH pre-loaded in separate tabs. Show results side-by-side.
- "Same AI. Different skill. That's the gap."

**TAKEAWAY:**

> "Same AI. Different skill. That's the gap."

**BACKUP PLAN:** If live demo fails, show pre-captured screenshots side-by-side.

**TRANSITION:** "So you've seen what one good prompt can do. Now let me show you what's actually POSSIBLE when you stack these skills."
